## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are approved Studio Parallel content strategists, founders and operators working
inside one shared internal workspace. They need to move between setup, evidence review, strategy and
operational recovery without exposing workspace data or provider credentials.

## Product Purpose

The product connects Studio Parallel's own Instagram performance observations to repeatable creative
characteristics, then turns that evidence into inspectable, non-causal strategy and next-video
recommendations. Success means an internal user can trace what the product says back to posts,
metric snapshots, analysis versions, assumptions and limitations.

## Positioning

This is an internal evidence system, not an ornamental social dashboard or an attempt to explain the
Instagram algorithm. Application code owns comparable statistics and evidence retrieval; model output
supplies structured creative observations and proposals that remain visibly distinct from measured
facts.

## Operating Context

The recurring operating pattern is inferred as **triage then act**: establish the current location,
surface blocking attention, and provide one safe next action. Users then inspect imported posts,
source video and transcripts, processing status, trends, strategy evidence, account health and safe
operational settings. Long-running work continues outside the browser and returns explicit queued,
processing, partial, failed, expired or insufficient-data states.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- V1 has one Studio Parallel workspace with server-authorised ownership on every data action.
- Primary destinations are Dashboard, Posts, Trends, Strategy and Operations; Instagram accounts,
  Settings and the session menu are secondary destinations.
- Missing metrics are first-class values and never become zero. Confidence, provenance, capture time,
  version metadata and limitations remain inspectable.
- The interface must not expose credential material, unauthorised resource names, private media or
  sensitive diagnostic content.
- Public onboarding, clients, billing, complex roles, publishing and public marketing navigation are
  excluded from this internal product.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name **Studio Parallel** and the canonical product terminology in
`docs/product/terminology.md`. The voice is direct, evidence-first and operationally honest: name what
failed, whether data is safe, and the next action without hype, causal claims or generic error copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains approved product definitions, user journeys, terminology, screen hierarchy,
design principles, technical security contracts and deterministic test fixtures. No approved logo,
photography, customer proof, benchmarks or production Instagram data are available; future surfaces
must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence and provenance precede interpretation.
2. Every state offers one safe, relevant next action.
3. Missing, partial and asynchronous work remain explicit rather than cosmetically smoothed over.
4. Scanability and keyboard flow outrank dashboard theatre.
5. Server authorisation is the boundary; client navigation is never treated as access control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web product requires semantic landmarks and headings, keyboard-operable navigation and actions,
visible focus, labelled controls, non-colour status cues, polite live regions, reduced-motion support
and sufficient contrast. Core setup, review and recovery flows must remain usable at a 390px viewport;
dense comparisons preserve meaning through labelled stacking or horizontal scrolling rather than
clipping or hiding essential actions.
